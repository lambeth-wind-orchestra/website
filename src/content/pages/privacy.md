This is the privacy notice for Lambeth Wind Orchestra. It explains what personal data we collect, and how and why we use it.

Lambeth Wind Orchestra is a registered charity in England and Wales (number 1138703). We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about what information we hold about you.

## What data we collect

We may collect personal data from you when you:

- Contact us via email or our website
- Sign up for our mailing list
- Join the orchestra as a member
- Purchase tickets for our events

This data may include your name, email address, phone number, and musical background.

## How we use your data

We use your data to:

- Respond to your enquiries
- Send you updates about upcoming LWO events (if you have opted in)
- Manage orchestra membership and rehearsals
- Process event bookings

## Sharing your data

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may use third-party services (like mailing list providers) to help us communicate with you, but they are only permitted to use your data as instructed by us.

## Your rights

You have the right to request a copy of the data we hold about you, and to ask us to correct or delete it at any time.

If you have any questions about our privacy policy, please [contact us](/contact).
